The Shape of a Quiet Interface

Essays · 104 words · Edited 2h ago

Make room for the sentence

The best tools do not ask to be admired. They hold a little space, keep the edges quiet, and let the work arrive without an audience.

A quiet interface is not an empty one. It is an interface with a clear intention: make the next sentence easier to begin.

The discipline of less

Every control has a cost. A label asks for a glance. A bright accent asks for a decision. When the furniture begins to compete with the room, we lose the feeling that the room belongs to us.

A small test

Remove one thing that feels merely reassuring. Keep the thing that helps a person make progress. The difference is often visible in the first minute.
